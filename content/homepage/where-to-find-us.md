We know that buying your food from smaller farms isn’t always the simplest thing. You have to go somewhere other than where you get most of your groceries, and sometimes it costs more.

However, these farms often grow a wider variety of crops using more sustainable practices, which means the food is fresher, more nutritionally dense, and grown closer to where you live. By choosing local, seasonal produce you’re not just getting better food, you’re also helping to create a more resilient and healthy food system in your community.

Thank you for supporting your local farmers, wherever you live.
